Rema

  • Date Of Birth: May 1, 2000
  • Stage Name: Rema Boy
  • Birth name: Divine Ikubor
  • First Song To Release: Dumebi (2019)

Divine Ikubor popularly known as "Rema" boy is a Nigerian singer, rapper, and songwriter born on May 1, 2000, in Benin City, Nigeria

He gained fame after a social media freestyle video caught the attention of artist D'Prince, which led to a record deal with Mavin Records.

Known for his "Afrorave" sound, a blend of afrobeats and trap, Rema released his debut album Rave & Roses in 2022.

Divine Ikubor was born into a Christian family in Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria. He is from Igbanke in Orhionmwon LGA of Edo state.

He discovered his passion for music during his secondary school days at Ighile Group of Schools in Edo State, where he began singing and rapping.

Wizkid

  • Date Of Birth: July 16, 1990
  • Stage Name: Wizkid
  • Birth Name: Ayodeji Ibrahim Balogun
  • First Song To Release: Holla At Your Boy 2010

Wizkid real name is "Ayodeji Ibrahim Balogun" he is a Nigerian singer and songwriter born on July 16, 1990, in Lagos.

He began his music career at age 11, formed a group called the Glorious Five, and later signed with Empire Mates Entertainment

(E.M.E.) in 2009, gaining fame with his 2011 debut album Superstar. He is celebrated for international hits like \"One Dance\"

with Drake and \"Essence\" with Tems and has won numerous awards, including a Grammy for his work on Beyoncé's \"Brown Skin Girl\".

Wizkid, is a Nigerian singer and songwriter. Born in the Ojuelegba suburb of Surulere, Lagos, Wizkid is a voice in the emerging Afrobeats movement.

His music is a blend of Afrobeats, afropop, R&B, afrobeat, reggae, dancehall, and pop. He began recording music at the age of 11 and released a collaborative album with the Glorious Five, a group he and a few of his church friends formed.

He rose to limelight after releasing "Holla at Your Boy", the lead single from his debut studio album, Superstar (2011), which also spawned the singles "Tease Me/Bad Guys" and "Don't Dull".

On 17 September 2014, he released the self-titled second studio album, Ayo, which was supported by six singles, including "Jaiye Jaiye" Read More...

Burna Boy

  • Date Of Birth: July 2, 1991
  • Stage Name: Burna Boy
  • Birth Name: Damini Ebunoluwa Ogulu
  • First Song To Release: Freedom Freestyle 2010

Burna Boy is a Nigerian singer-songwriter born Damini Ebunoluwa Ogulu on July 2, 1991, in Port Harcourt.

He gained recognition in 2012 with his single "Like to Party" and is known for his "Afro-fusion" style, blending Afrobeats with other genres. His career highlights include winning a Grammy Award for Best Global Music Album in 2021 for Twice as Tall and becoming one of the most successful African artists globally.

In 2019, Burna Boy won Best International Act at the BET Awards and was named an Apple Music Up Next artist. That same year, he released his fourth

studio album, African Giant, which won Album of the Year at the 2019 All Africa Music Awards and was nominated for Best World Music Album at the 62nd Annual Grammy Awards.

In 2020, Burna Boy won African Artist of the Year at the Ghana Music Awards, and released his fifth studio album Twice as Tall, which won Best World Music Album at the 63rd Annual Grammy Awards. He won Best International Act at the 2021 BET Awards for the third Read More...

Davido

  • Date Of Birth: November 21, 1992
  • Stage name: Davido
  • Birth name: David Adedeji Adeleke
  • First Song To Release: Back When 2011

David Adedeji Adeleke (born November 21, 1992), known as Davido, is an American-born Nigerian singer, songwriter, and record producer who rose to fame in 2011 with his hit single "Dami Duro".

He is known for blending Afrobeats with pop and hip-hop, and founded the record label Davido Music Worldwide (DMW). His successful albums include A Good Time (2019) and A Better Time (2020), which featured international artists like Nicki Minaj and Lil Baby.

Davido was born in Atlanta, U.S, and raised in Lagos, Nigeria. He made his music debut as a member of the group KB International. Davido studied business administration at Oakwood University.

Before dropping out to make beats and record vocal references. He rose to fame after releasing "Dami Duro", the second single from his debut studio album Omo Baba Olowo (2012), from which six additional singles—"Back When", "Ekuro", "Overseas", "All of You", "Gbon Gbon" and "Feel Alright"—were taken.

In 2012, Davido won the Next Rated award at The Headies. Between 2013 and 2015, he released the singles "Gobe", "One of a Kind", "Skelewu", "Aye", "Tchelete (Goodlife)", "Naughty", "Owo Ni Koko", "The Sound" and "The Money" Read More...
